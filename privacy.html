WOTFit by Ken Kanfix

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Overview

WOTFit is a local-first workout tracker. The workouts, sets, settings and history you create are stored on your own device. WOTFit does not require an account and does not upload your training data to a server.

Data stored on your device

WOTFit stores the following information locally in your browser or app storage:

  • Workout history, sets, reps, weights and notes.
  • Training goal, experience level, equipment, units and rest preferences.
  • Body weight and calorie estimates you enter or generate.
  • HIIT timer settings and exercise selections.

This information stays on your device unless you choose to export a backup. Exported files are saved wherever you choose to save them.

Collection and sharing

  • WOTFit does not collect personal information for the developer.
  • WOTFit does not include analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • WOTFit does not sell or share your workout data.
  • Rest and HIIT alerts are generated locally on your device.

If you use the web version, it is served as static files by a hosting provider. That provider may process standard request logs, but your workout data is not included in those requests.

Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove all workouts and settings from the Settings screen with Reset all data, or clear the app's storage from your device settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data. Because the developer does not receive your data, there is no developer-held data to request or delete.

Export a backup before resetting, clearing storage, uninstalling or switching devices if you want to keep your history.

Security

Your data is stored in the app's local storage and is protected by your device's security. WOTFit does not transmit your training data over the internet. Keep your device locked and back up your export files if you need them.

Children

WOTFit is intended for a general fitness audience and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party services

WOTFit does not integrate third-party analytics or advertising services. Google Play and your device platform may process information related to app downloads, updates and payments under their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when WOTFit's features or legal requirements change. The current version will always be available at this URL, with the date shown above.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Ken Kanfix at kenkanfix@gmail.com. Please include “WOTFit privacy” in the subject line.
